✦ How the model works — open & genuine

Platforms run referral and partner programmes because acquiring customers through trusted recommendations is more efficient than pure advertising. When a contact completes the required steps (eKYC, activation, sometimes a first trade), the platform allocates a reward or commission.

You earn a clear, displayed share (typically shown as ₹100–200 per successful referral where applicable). A coordination share of approximately 30% covers tracking, support, and network operations. The platform continues to benefit from the lifetime value of the new customer. This is standard industry practice — not a loophole and not designed to shock anyone.

Earnings depend only on successful, verified outcomes. This page is in a testing phase. Amounts, available platforms, and processes may be refined as real usage and platform policies evolve. Referral links that are not yet live are marked “Available soon”.
